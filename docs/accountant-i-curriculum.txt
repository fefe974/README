Accountant I — Detailed Curriculum
County Finance Department · Application-first, textbook-
driven, mobile-delivered, agent-coached
Source text: Reck, Lowensohn & Neely, Accounting for Governmental & Nonprofit Entities,
18th ed. — cited as [GNP]. Companion documents: v1 (design logic), v2 (assignment
mapping), v3 (mobile architecture), v4 (agent layer), Coach Agent Behavior Spec.
This is the build document. Every unit is named, numbered, sourced, and timed.
How to read this
Unit ID scheme. W3.L2 = Week 3, Lesson 2. Formats: L lesson (7–10 min, phone), P
practice (12–20 min, phone), C case (8–12 min, phone), V voice explain (2–3 min each,
phone), D drill (3–5 min ×2 daily, phone), M milestone (60–120 min, off-phone).
Source column. E/P 4-20 = Exercise/Problem 4-20 from the text. Q 4-10 = Question
4-10. Case 4-13 = Case 4-13. LO4-1 = the chapter’s own learning objective.
★ marks a graded milestone. ⚑ marks a currency patch — content where the 2019 text is
superseded and the lesson must correct it.
Weekly load: ~75 min on phone + one off-phone milestone of 60–120 min + reading. Total
~6.5–8 hrs inside a normal work week.
Program totals: 16 weeks · 58 lessons · 91 practice sets · 16 cases · 47 voice items · 16
milestones · 604 drill cards · 11 graded milestones + 1 capstone.
Week 0 — Environment and orientation
Nothing is taught. Everything is assembled. Do not start Week 1 until all six are done.
